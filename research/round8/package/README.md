## What cannot be independently verified from this package, and why
1. That the production server actually served what its rows and tapes say. The rows were pulled from the server's own public endpoints (/predictions, /tape, /health); the package includes the server source whose hash /health reports, and the exact replay reproduces the served states from the recorded inputs (8,099 of 8,100), but a reader cannot re-observe the past.
2. The exposure table's derivation. It was built from 92 million one-second BTC/USDC closes (public Binance data, 2023-10-02 … 2026-09-01) that are not included (size); the builder scripts and the table's by-year exceedance statistics are. The table's values can only be checked against a fresh download.
3. The round-7 TEST/FIT replay figures (e.g., 0 of 4,452). The traces are large and not included; their summary outputs are. They are cited as context, not as this comparison's evidence.
4. Deployment facts from Render (deploy id, auto-deploy setting) come from the owner's authenticated account; a third party can verify only the public /health endpoint and the public GitHub branches.
5. The Nov 11 incident has no production rows (lost with a machine replaced 2026-11-13); its entry facts come from the owner's observer and two engine reconstructions included under outputs/round7.
Kalshi results are verifiable by anyone: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/markets/<ticker> (fields result, expiration_value, floor_strike).

No credentials, keys or account identifiers (configuration names appear in CONFIG.md without values). No personal trade history, balances, orders or positions of the owner. The quotes in the tapes are Kalshi's public order books.
