## 5. Is the exposure floor proportionate?
Mandatory integrity checks (not tunable): input-age budget, venue quorum, coverage hard gate, strike chain (I2); missing samples and fail-closed official reference inside the minute (b8/b9). Discretionary threshold: θ = 0.05 % (two-sided) chosen before evaluation (PR11_DECLARATION §2) because the 99.9 ¢ break-even after fee is 0.09 % and 0.05 % two-sided ≈ 0.025 % adverse leaves a factor ≈ 3.6 for basis error, feed staleness and a worse future. Protection it demonstrates: every qualifying loss withheld (Nov 11 reconstruction, Nov 14 direct replay, Nov 15 production rows), 0 of 4,452 TEST entries (upper 0.067 %), 0 of 401 live (upper 0.74 %). Opportunities removed: first-entry offers 173 → 7; windows with any available admitted second 179 → 10 of 212. Already-evaluated alternatives on this denominator (reconstruction only; exploratory, not for selection): θ = 0.2 %: 55 windows with an available admitted second, both losses withheld, 1 TEST loss (round 7); θ = 0.1 %: 32, both withheld, 0 TEST losses; θ = 0.05 % (frozen): 10; θ = 0.025 %: 8, withholds one winner too. The existing evidence does not establish that a materially less restrictive table gives comparable protection: the 0.1 % table's zero losses are on examined data and a live sample cannot bound 0.09 %. UNRESOLVED. The one bounded comparison that could inform it, without deployment: a pre-registered shadow evaluation on the hourly tape rows and prediction pulls from a fixed start date, θ = 0.05 % (primary) versus θ = 0.1 % (comparator), counting losses among the comparator's exclusive entries and available offers, stopping at 60 days or 600 exclusive windows; it can reject a comparator rate ≥ 0.5 % but cannot establish the 0.09 % break-even, which rests on the historical table either way. No threshold search is proposed.

## 8. Decision
**Supported protective safeguard, but practical trading usefulness remains unproven.** Build 9 withholds exactly the class in which the deployed server lost (90–120 s entries with 15–18 bps cushions), in direct replay for Nov 14 and by reconstruction for Nov 11 and Nov 15, and changes nothing else in the engine; its live record is consistent with the historical bound (0 of 401) but cannot by itself distinguish it from the baseline (2 of 403). At the owner's entry rule the candidate's first qualifying second carries an available offer in 7 of 200 windows with a book (the baseline: 173 of 202); any admitted second with an offer exists in 10 of 212 windows, almost always at 99.9 ¢ with ≈ 0.1 ¢ per contract of edge, so the usable opportunity is about one window in twenty and its expected value cannot be verified at the sizes that matter (one loss at 80,000 contracts ≈ $79,900 against ≈ $75 per win). Unresolved risks: a move larger than the 2023–2026 history reverses an admitted call; the live-index substitution for the newest print is an approximation the historical evaluation did not exercise; monitoring and rollback depend on this session; sparse quotes may vanish within 1–3 s of the admitted second. Owner actions: decide whether a safeguard that withholds ≈ 96 % of the baseline's first-entry offers is what should run live; if so, the approval statement v7 for build 9 stands and a future attended window must be named; if a less restrictive table is of interest, approve the pre-registered shadow comparison in §5 (no deployment); choose I4 explicitly. No deployment, publication, trading, configuration change or new scheduled task was made.
