## 1. The strongest entry-protection approach identified
PR11, the settlement-exposure gate: keep the existing LOCKED reading and permit entry only at seconds where the adverse move of the still-unknown settlement prints needed to reverse the settlement (R = 60·|gap| / remaining prints inside the minute; |gap| before it) is at least R*(h), the move that occurred with a frequency of at most 0.05 % (two-sided) over every second of the 2023-10 … 2026-08 one-second history at that horizon, in every volatility regime (68 bps at 120 s, 54 at 90, 35 at 60, 25 at 30, 16 at 10). It uses the exact contract arithmetic and the recorded official prints; it does not use the volatility estimate and changes no probability. Mathematical certainty while trading remains possible is not achievable for this contract (YES is certain only when the recorded prints alone exceed 60·K — a 1.7 % cushion with one print left; NO is never certain before the last print); PR11 is the achievable option (b).

## 2. Whether it meets the requirement, and where it falls short
Met, on the frozen criteria: C1 protection bound (TEST Sep 1–Nov 12 2026 at the measured scale: 0 losses in 6,299 entries by path outcome, upper-95 % 0.0475 %; 0 in 4,452 by official outcome at cushions ≥ 5 bps, upper 0.067 %; 0 in 4,456–4,457 on the two other cohorts; 0 of 85 on Nov 13–15); C2 prevention (0 of the baseline's 9 losses admitted); C3 usability (10 of 170 live tape windows have an admitted second with an executable offer — 5.9 %, the floor was 5 %). The three qualifying losses (Nov 11, Nov 14, Nov 15) would not have been admitted at any second before their moves, under the exact and the displayed rule.
Short of the requirement in these respects: (i) usability is low — the gate admits seconds in 159 of 170 windows, but at those seconds the called side usually has no offer at all, so the usable opportunity is about one window in seventeen, almost always at 99.9 ¢ (edge ≈ 0.09 ¢ per contract per win before the small residual risk); (ii) the live sample (351 PR11 entries, 0 lost) cannot by itself bound the rate below 0.85 %; the bound rests on the three-year exceedance table and the 2026 separated replay; (iii) the protection is a frequency bound, not certainty, and a larger-than-history move still reverses; (iv) the replay cannot resolve cushions below 5 bps (judged on 61 live entries, 0 lost); (v) PR11 does not apply beyond 120 s to close, where LOCKED shows as today.
Stronger protection as opportunity falls, measured: TEST losses under the four frozen tables 1 / 0 / 0 / 0 (θ 0.2 / 0.1 / 0.05 / 0.025 %) against 51 / 31 / 10 / 8 executable live windows. The 0.1 % table had the same zero losses with three times the usable entries; it was not selected (selection on the evaluation data is barred) and would be a new specification with its own separated test.

## 6. Next supported experiment (only because it can change the decision)
