# PR11 — settlement-exposure gate: specification and acceptance criteria, FROZEN before any evaluation on separated data
Written 2026-11-16 (clock read at the time of the EXECUTION_LOG line that records this file's sha256). Round 7, owner's directive sections 3–5.

## 0. What PR11 is, in one sentence
Keep the existing LOCKED reading unchanged, and permit entry only at seconds where the adverse move of the still-unknown settlement prints that would be needed to reverse the settlement has, over the whole 2023-10 … 2026-08 one-second history and every volatility regime in it, occurred with a frequency of at most 0.05 % (two-sided). It is a NEW specification (the owner's clarified objective), not a revival of any rejected candidate.

## 1. Rule (exact)
At every decision second in the final 120 s of a KXBTC15M window, with K = strike, gap = expected settlement − K as the server computes it (lockGeometry25: the recorded official prints enter exactly, the unknown prints at the current index), n = official prints recorded, τ = seconds to close:
* if n ≥ 1 and τ ≤ 60: h = 60 − n (at least 1), R = |gap| / K × 10⁴ × 60 / (60 − n) (bps);
* otherwise: h = min(120, ⌈τ⌉), R = |gap| / K × 10⁴ (bps).
PR11 ADMITS the second iff (a) state is LOCKED or PROVEN LOCKED, (b) pFlip ≤ 0.002 (exact rule; the displayed variant uses pFlip < 0.0025 and is evaluated separately as "disp"), and (c) R ≥ R*(h).
R*(h), h = 1…120, is the table in `round7/qtable/rstar_0.050.json` (sha256 87f9f1c29b19f5433b93e4677320bd72cd5e4abe99730e16ec899ced5cbcf5b4): the smallest integer R such that the fraction of seconds t in 2023-10-02 … 2026-09-01 (92,016,000 seconds of 1-s BTC/USDC closes, Binance public data, provenance in replay_pub/data/sec/PROVENANCE_binance_BTCUSDC.ndjson) with |mean(settlement-type prints over horizon h) − p[t]| / p[t] ≥ R bps is ≤ 0.05 %; for h ≤ 60 the mean of the next h prints, for h > 60 the mean of the 60 prints ending at t + h; monotone non-decreasing in h (no adjustment was needed). Values: h = 1: 10, 5: 13, 10: 16, 20: 21, 30: 25, 45: 31, 60: 35, 75: 46, 90: 54, 105: 62, 120: 68 bps. Builder: `round7/qtable/build_q_full.py` (sha256 37e5ef459af082dc2ebca1e78a16c96d48b3efc3264db6fb6d9c1d96b6c4fb45), source table `q_full_usdc_fit.json` (sha256 3e5e4e49ada15f5416529aca17e4ba9c00d75c1697382d479303838825553755), table writer `make_rstar.py`.
In replay traces (no n field) n is taken as max(0, 60 − ⌈τ⌉) for τ ≤ 60, i.e. h = ⌈τ⌉; production n can differ from this by one print.
Entry under PR11 = the first admitted second of the window (one entry per window), on the leader's side at that second.

## 2. Why θ = 0.05 % (fixed now, not to be moved after results)
At 99.9 ¢ the break-even loss rate after the default fee is 0.09 % (at 99.8 ¢ 0.18 %). The two-sided 0.05 % corresponds to about 0.025 % on the adverse side, leaving a factor of ~3.6 below the 99.9 ¢ break-even for basis error, feed staleness and a worse-than-history future. Three other tables (θ = 0.2 %, 0.1 %, 0.025 %; sha256 c7663e3a…1599, 1617d22f…185d, cf97dd3e…61ff) are frozen for the sensitivity curve only; no table is selected or re-selected on the evaluation data.

## 5. Acceptance criteria (frozen; all on the exact rule unless stated)
* C1 PROTECTION BOUND (TEST primary cohort): among PR11 entries, the exact one-sided 95 % upper bound of the loss rate is ≤ 0.09 % with proxy-defined outcomes (path risk), AND ≤ 0.09 % with official outcomes among entries with raw cushion ≥ 5 bps.
* C2 PREVENTION RELATIVE TO THE BASELINE (TEST primary, official outcomes, entries with cushion ≥ 5 bps): of the baseline's losing windows, PR11 admitted a losing entry in at most one third; evaluable only if the baseline has ≥ 3 such losses (else "not evaluable", reported).
* C3 USABILITY (live tape Nov 13–16): at least 5 % of tape windows, and at least 8 windows, have ≥ 1 PR11-admitted second at which the called side had an executable offer (ask ≤ 99.9 ¢, size ≥ 10 contracts, book received ≤ 3 s before the row). The ask and time-left distributions are reported.
* C4 ROBUSTNESS (reported, must not contradict C1): C1 on the secondary cohorts (unscaled, 0.866, Nov 13–15) and the displayed variant; the sensitivity curve over the four θ tables.
* C5 INCIDENTS (diagnostic, reported): each qualifying loss classified Entry prevented / Entry still admitted / Warning after entry only / Unresolved under the exact and displayed variants, chronologically, with every later admitted second before the move listed.
PR11 QUALIFIES iff C1, C2 (when evaluable) and C3 all hold. If C2 is not evaluable: "qualifies on the protection bound; prevention effect not demonstrable on TEST". If any of C1/C3 fails: FAILED on its frozen criteria; no re-selection of θ on TEST or live; the next step is recorded, not taken.

## 6. What a pass would and would not establish
A pass establishes that admitted seconds carry a regime-agnostic historical exceedance ≤ 0.05 %, that the policy's realised losses on separated 2026 data are consistent with that bound, and that such seconds coincide with executable offers often enough to be a usable policy. It does not establish certainty (a move larger than any in the record can still reverse a settlement), it does not identify the cause of the November cluster, and it is not a validation of the engine's probabilities. A release would additionally require the implemented gate to reproduce the production state exactly in off/shadow mode, a self-test suite, an exact tape replay, and the owner's approval of that exact release; and a new prospective discriminator for the PR11 population.
