## (a) Is the sigma scaling a comparability correction or a re-tuning?
A1 MATERIAL — legitimate in direction and size, not a re-tuning, but not a clean equivalence. On the 202 common windows (S/round5/transfer/usdc_12_I2.trace.ndjson vs S/r30/live/scoreboard/rows) the proxy/live raw-sigma ratio is 1.184 (p10 1.08, p90 1.29) and 1.16–1.24 in every regime bucket (implied scale 0.83–0.885 around the 0.866 used). The pf ratio, however, is 0.78–1.16 in buckets 1–3 and 1.55–1.85 in buckets 4–6: the bucket is assigned from the raw (unscaled) sigma and the recalibration ratios differ by bucket, so "a live 0.2 % ≈ a replay 0.31 %" holds only in active regimes. The threshold-equivalent check (pf ≤ 0.0031) is therefore the same adjustment through another lever, not independent corroboration, and it misses the quiet-regime loss (Sep 6, bk 2) for exactly this reason.
A2 MATERIAL — the "comparable rate" is a bracket. Threshold sweep on the unscaled year traces (review_tmp/thr_sweep.txt): [90,120] losses 0/7,281 (pf ≤ .0020), 0/7,723 (.0023), 1/8,116 (.0026), 2/8,675 (.0031), 3/8,958 (.0034), 4/9,344 (.0038), 9/9,960 (.0045). ## Verdict
Sections 20–21: supported (counts, overlap, engine identity, the 1.18 ratio); their P-values are approximations of the right sign. Section 22: the qualitative claim — the 0-in-7,281 cohort was stricter than production and the comparable historical rate is above zero — is supported; the quantitative claims are not: the comparable rate is a method-dependent bracket 0.01–0.14 % whose top end is the one relied on, its "pre-registered prediction" is unverifiable and foreseeable, the cushion-matched sub-rates are confounded, and "doubled from September" is a November cluster. The headline "the discrepancy does not survive a comparable analysis" overstates: the exact discrepancy moves from P = 0.003 (vs 0/7,281) to P = 0.02–0.19 depending on baseline, and is P ≈ 0.03 on disjoint windows against the most lenient baseline — reduced, not removed. Section 23: correctly specified and computed, but a weak instrument over the plausible range (coin-flip to resolve at 0.2–0.3 %) with an H0 at the top of the bracket. Section 28: consistent with the engine-level ratio; the "4 % above fit scale" does not change p0. Nothing here supports a threshold change, and nothing here supports calling the live cluster explained.
Single most important caveat: every rate in these sections rests on 2 live and 3–4 historical events, and the historical ones are synthetic entries (real outcomes) at an equivalence scale known to ±5 %, across which the historical loss count moves 0 → 4; "comparable" should be read as "bracketed", and the replay's own November cluster (6 in 1,043, P = 0.0007) says the baseline may be moving in the very period the discriminator will sample.
