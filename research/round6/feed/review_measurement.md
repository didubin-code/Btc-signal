# Adversarial review of the feed-scale measurement (record section 28; round6/feed/)

Disclosure: this review was written by an instance of the same model family as the author, briefed by the author. Its independence is limited to not having written the code; it recomputed every number below from the raw inputs with its own scripts (round6/feed/review_tmp/: recompute.py -> recompute.txt, ring_check.py -> ring_check.txt, period2_decomp.py -> period2_decomp.txt, tercile_vwap.py -> tercile_vwap.txt). No existing file was modified; no production service was touched.

Short form: the central number reproduces (rows sigma/1.03 over the rebuilt fit-type sigma: median 1.013, n = 32,069) and the estimator chain is exact, so "no constant multiplier is justified" and "production reads about 4 % above the fit scale" stand UNDER THE DOCUMENTED CONSTRUCTION. Two stated things are wrong (the explanation of section 27's 1.14, and "within 1-2 % at every horizon and regime"), and the result is far more sensitive to how the lost fit feed was built than the record says.

## Findings

1. Timestamp alignment - MINOR (wording only; the pairing is right). The BRTI stamp is on an integer second (sourceTs % 1000 = 0 in all 50,648 tape rows) and marks the END of the second it summarises: fit-type(t) vs BRTI(t+1) correlates 0.744 (period 2, n = 1,967) and 0.592 (period 1, rows spot, n = 30,179) versus <= 0.21 at other lags, independent of the Binance kline convention. That convention is open-time (fetch_binance_1s.py:45-49), and fit-type(t) vs Binance(t) is 0.786 at lag 0, so the author's "kline t vs print t+1 = 0.71" (recomputed 0.712) is the same statement. The decision tick in second S sits at S + 0.01..0.42 s (p10/p50/p90 12/220/416 ms, not "t + 0.4"); it correlates 0.790 with the print stamped S and 0.881 with the fit-type return of second S-1, i.e. both reflect the market of [S-1, S). None of this affects an RMS ratio, which is alignment-free. (build_pairs.py:22-31, analyze_pairs.py:59-72)

2. Deduplication - MATERIAL (the explanation offered for section 27 is wrong; the dedup itself is sound). Prints are keyed by seq and placed at their source second correctly, and the value recorded on the tape equals the rows' spot at the same second (|diff| median 0.000 bps, n = 38,991). But the record's claim that "BRTI reads 1.14 x live" was "an artefact of re-used prints" (WORK_RECORD.md section 28, first sentence) does not survive recomputation: the per-window median of BRTI/composite 1-s RMS is 1.141 on the per-row (re-used) series and 1.142 on the de-duplicated series (167 / 148 windows). A re-used print produces a zero return and a 2-s catch-up return, which with lag-1 autocorrelation +0.08 inflates a pooled RMS by sqrt(1 + 0.28 x 0.08) ~ 1 %, not 8 %. The 0.947 in section 28 is a different statistic (pooled, variance-weighted; 1/0.947 = 1.056; my pooled per-row 1.070, dedup 1.058), not a corrected one. The "28 % of rows carry the previous print" is also inflated by counting the 2,100 own_capture_2 rows and the pull/_new rows twice across overlapping tape files (build_pairs.py:13-34; first-row-per-second gives 8,573 of 50,048 = 17 %). Missing prints: 17.2 % of composite seconds have no unique print (0.5 % Nov 13, 6.4 % Nov 14, 11.7 % Nov 15, 28.5 % Nov 16), runs are mostly length 1 (2,333 of 2,537), and the excluded seconds are more active (composite 1-s RMS 0.934 vs 0.865 bps). Ratios on common seconds are unbiased by this; levels and the Nov 16 subset are not.

3. Estimator fidelity - MINOR (verified exact). Rows' `sigma` is geo.sigmaBps (server line 3294) = m25SigmaBps x 1.03 x inflation (lines 1543-1575, 1619-1622). Reproducing it from the tape's 1 Hz ring samples with the server's ringRmsVol (500 ms cut, dt floor, L/3 threshold) and the inflation formula (age = oldest used venue quote, line 4261) gives rows sigma / reproduced = 1.0000 (p5 0.9999, p95 1.0001) on 4,800 rows (ring_check.txt). fit_scale.py:33-41 matches the definition (simple returns, bps, same thresholds). Inflation left in by dividing by 1.03 alone: median 1.0002 (tau 240-300), 1.0005 (90-120), 1.0018 (30-90), 1.0089 with p99 1.34 (tau < 30, n = 4,525); the author's "<90" bucket mixes those in, which is why it reads 1.018 vs 1.011. Removing it on period 2 moves the median from 1.012 to 1.009. Two small omissions: (i) the ring reads 1.5-3 % above the decision-tick composite at 1 s (ring/tick 1.017, 1.029, 1.014 on three tapes, with dt), so tables (b) and (c) of fit_scale.txt and table C of analyze_pairs.txt, which use the tick series, understate what the model consumes at 1 s by that much (the (a) ratio already includes it); (ii) after a boot the 900-s window runs on 300-540 samples for ~5 min (300 of 2,100 period-2 rows) and no restart check exists for period 1. Neither moves the result.

4. Fit-type construction - MATERIAL (the conclusion is conditional on one metadata string). The reconstruction matches index_compare.py:11-30 and the fit string "Coinbase 1.0 / Kraken 0.8 last-trade proxy, 1 Hz" (server line 1536). Choices that are NOT in the string do not matter: infinite staleness, both-venues-required and last-trade-vs-kline-close all give an identical median 1.013 (Kraken has no trade in 43.5 % of seconds but is stale > 30 s in only 6 of 97,855). Choices that are in the string, or are plausible alternatives to it, matter a lot (rows sigma/1.03 over the variant's sigma, n = 32,069): Coinbase-only 0.881, Kraken-only 0.939, equal weights 1.018, per-second VWAP of the same trades 1.144. On the same seconds the 1-s RMS is 0.787 bps for the two-venue last-trade mix, 0.904 Coinbase alone, 0.851 Kraken alone, 0.794 composite, 0.986 Binance: two noisy last-trade legs averaged happen to land on the composite's scale. So "at the scale the model was fitted on" rests on the owner's lost store having been exactly a last-price, 1.0/0.8 blend; a per-second average or a single venue would move the answer by -12 % to +14 %.

5. Selection - MINOR. Rows exist for Nov 12 21:40 - Nov 16 12:29 but trade files only for Nov 12 21:25 - Nov 14 00:36, so the loss days (Nov 15-16) cannot be compared with the fit-type. Within the covered days the loss conditions reproduce the headline: regime 3-4 and tau 90-120: median 1.013 (p10 0.935, p90 1.058, n = 1,193); plus cushion 10-25 bps: 1.016 (n = 525); cushion 15-18 bps: 1.018 (n = 95); any regime, 90-120 s, 15-18 bps: 1.017 (n = 230). By day: Nov 12 1.041 (n = 2,976), Nov 13 1.011 (n = 28,493), Nov 14 0.980 (n = 600). Transfer to Nov 15-16 is supported only indirectly: composite/BRTI at 30-60 s is 1.015-1.048 on every day, while at 1 s it swings 0.756 (Nov 13) / 0.986 / 0.981 / 0.828 (Nov 16).

6. Regime dependence - MATERIAL for the precision claim, not for the direction. On the same period-1 seconds (rows idx, rows spot, fit-type; tercile_vwap.txt): calm tercile comp/BRTI 0.687 at 1 s, fit-type/BRTI 0.761, comp/fit-type 0.903; at 2 s 0.968; at 5 s 1.009; at 30 s 1.000. By model bucket, comp/fit-type at 1 s is 0.951 (buckets 1-3), 0.959 (4), 0.963 (5), 0.995 (6) and 1.00-1.04 at 5-30 s. Lag-1 autocorrelation: BRTI -0.091 / -0.070 / +0.025 (calm / middle / active), composite +0.15 to +0.20, fit-type +0.13 to +0.17, Binance ~0. So the "two-second incorporation" reading is right for the composite AND the fit-type index shares it (same sign and size, which is why the model's calibration absorbs it), but the record's "none shows the negative autocorrelation of i.i.d. noise" is wrong for BRTI in calm blocks (a ~9 % noise share at 1 s), and the sampler phase adds the 1.5-3 % of finding 3. The honest statement is: the composite is within about +/-5 % of the fit-type scale in the loss regime depending on statistic (per-decision window ratio +1 to +2 %; same-second pooled 1-s ratio -4 to -5 % for the tick, roughly -2 to -3 % for the ring), not "within 1-2 % at every horizon and regime".

7. Settlement quantity - MINOR (adequate and robust). The 60-s change of the trailing 60-print mean is a reasonable proxy for the innovation of the settlement average at 60-120 s; recomputed comp/BRTI 1.015 with BRTI shifted -1 / 0 / +1 s (corr 0.999, 0.999, 0.998; n ~ 17,000). Plain 90-s and 120-s returns: 1.013 and 1.011. The direct quantity, mean of the prints stamped close-59..close minus the index at close-100, gives 1.001 (corr 0.998) on the 102 window closes available. The settlement window definition (prints stamped close-59..close inclusive) matches the server's accumulator (line 3010) and the fit metadata ("align incl").

8. Conclusion-changing errors - none found under the documented construction. "Production reads about 4 % above the fit scale" = 1.013 x 1.03 = 1.043 with inflation <= 0.1 % at 90-120 s: correct. "A constant multiplier is not justified": this measurement cannot support 1.17 (it would require composite/fit-type ~ 0.88, which only the Coinbase-only variant gives), but with +/-3 % day-to-day and +/-5 % by statistic it also cannot exclude a few per cent either way. Adjacent number that is understated: Binance/fit-type at 1 s on the fit-period seconds is 0.986/0.787 = 1.25 (n = 31,904), not the "+17 %" quoted against BRTI, so the replay's sigma-scaled factor 0.866 (section 28, "1/1.16") is an under-correction of the replay proxy's scale; this bears on sections 20-22, not on the live claim.

## Verdict
The measurement is competently built and its core arithmetic is right: the rows' sigma is exactly the ring-based model definition times 1.03 times a negligible inflation, the rebuilt fit-type index is the documented one, and on 32,069 decision-seconds the live reading sits at 1.01 of the fit scale overall and 1.01-1.02 in the loss cells (regime 3-4, 90-120 s, 15-18 bps cushion), with the composite and the fit-type index sharing the same short-horizon smoothing signature (RMS_30/RMS_1 = 1.37 vs 1.35; BRTI 1.13; Binance 1.09). That is enough to withdraw LIVE_VOL_SCALE ~ 1.17 as a measured correction and to keep 1.03 as mildly conservative. The record should nevertheless be amended on three points: section 27's 1.14 was not a re-used-print artefact but a per-window median that survives de-duplication (1.142), driven by calm windows where BRTI's 1-s RMS carries noise and the composite's is smoothed; the precision is +/-5 %, not 1-2 %; and BRTI's calm-regime returns do show the negative autocorrelation the record says nothing shows.

Single most important caveat: the whole result is conditional on the lost fit feed having been exactly "last trade, Coinbase 1.0 / Kraken 0.8" as the one-line metadata says; the no-trade and staleness choices are harmless (identical 1.013), but a single-venue series would put the composite 12 % below the fit scale (which would justify a ~1.13 multiplier) and a per-second VWAP would put it 14 % above, and nothing in this container can distinguish those from the documented construction.
