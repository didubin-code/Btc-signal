## Restart and state-loss effects
The deploy restarts the observer once: an obs.gap of about 10–40 s (restart plus the /predictions catch-up from production's memory, which refills every row — nothing lost there); Kalshi book snapshots during those seconds are lost (recorded, not reconstructed); production is unaffected.
