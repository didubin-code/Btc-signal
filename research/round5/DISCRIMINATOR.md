# Pre-registered prospective discriminator for the [90,120]-s loss excess (written 2026-11-16 about 08:12 UTC, probabilities corrected 08:14 UTC by the clock, before any new data; sha256 recorded in WORK_RECORD section 23)
## Question
Is the live conditional loss frequency of the owner's entry class at 90–120 s left higher than its historical replay frequency, or were the two losses of Nov 13–16 a chance cluster?
## Eligible population and denominator (fixed)
One observation = one 15-minute market (window) with an observer primary-rule v1 entry (`rules/primary_v1.json`, id pinradar-primary-v1: the first production /predictions row with 0 < tau ≤ 120 s, state LOCKED or PROVEN LOCKED, pFlip ≤ 0.002 inclusive; `existing-lock` and `new-crossing` both count; `unknown-prior` counts) whose entry tau satisfies 90.0 ≤ tau ≤ 120.0 s, whose outcome is a finalized Kalshi result (`won` true or false), and whose window closes after 2026-11-16T07:00:00Z (the last window inspected when this was written). Windows with `not-determinable` primary status, no entry, or an entry at tau < 90 s are not in the population. Nothing before the cut-off is counted, whatever it shows. The denominator N is the count of such windows, read from the observer's window lines (`signal CORRECT`/`signal WRONG` with the entry's tau) at each look; `signal WRONG` = loss.
## Hypotheses (fixed)
H0 (history holds): the loss frequency p of this population is ≤ p0 = 0.041 % — the exact 95 % upper bound of the replay's 0 losses in 7,281 exact-rule [90,120] entries on real Kalshi settlements, 2026 (round5/compare/cohorts.txt). Taking the upper bound as H0 is conservative towards H0 (the point estimate is 0).
H1 (the label is wrong for this class): p ≥ p1 = 0.5 %, the LOCKED threshold itself — the level at which a "≤ 0.2 %" reading for this class is wrong by at least the whole LOCKED margin. The Nov 2–16 observed rate, 2 of 417 = 0.48 %, is near p1.
## Looks, horizon and decision thresholds (fixed; Poisson/binomial, one-sided)
Looks are taken when N first reaches 400, 800 and 1,600 eligible windows (at the current rate of ~29 eligible entries a day: about 14, 28 and 55 days). Cumulative losses K at each look decide:
- Look 1 (N = 400): REJECT H0 if K ≥ 2 [P(K ≥ 2 | p0) = 0.012]. Otherwise continue.
- Look 2 (N = 800): REJECT H0 if K ≥ 3 [P(K ≥ 3 | p0, 800) = 0.0046]. ACCEPT H0's adequacy if K = 0 [P(K = 0 | p1, 800) = e^(−4) = 0.018]. Otherwise continue.
- Look 3 (N = 1,600): REJECT H0 if K ≥ 4 [P(K ≥ 4 | p0, 1,600) = 0.0046]; ACCEPT if K ≤ 1 [P(K ≤ 1 | p1, 1,600) = 0.003]; K = 2 or 3 at this look = UNRESOLVED and the test ends (the rate is then estimated with its interval; neither hypothesis is excluded).
Overall probability of a false rejection under H0 across the three looks is 0.017 (200,000-run simulation, round5/DISCRIMINATOR_check.txt); power to reject under H1 is 0.59 at look 1, 0.76 by look 2, 0.96 by look 3. (An earlier draft of this file, before any data, misstated the look-2 and look-3 tail probabilities; corrected 08:14 UTC from the simulation, thresholds unchanged.) The thresholds will not be changed after a loss. No other look is taken; the hourly and 6-hourly readings report K and N but do not decide.
## Previously inspected data
Everything up to and including the window closing 2026-11-16T07:00Z (Nov 2–16: 417 entries, 2 losses at [90,120]) was examined before this specification and is excluded from K and N. The bin [90,120] itself was chosen after seeing where the two losses fell; the test above is therefore the first clean test of that bin.
## What each outcome justifies
- REJECT H0: the historical zero-loss cohort is not evidence of live safety for this class; the ≤ 0.2 % reading at 90–120 s left is not supported live. Justified then: a prospectively declared entry-side correction for this class (declared and evaluated under a frozen protocol before use; PR10 stays rejected and is not revived), and the owner's own choice whether to keep entering this class meanwhile. Not justified: any change claimed to be validated by the rejection itself.
- ACCEPT: the live rate is consistent with history at the p1 level; the two losses are treated as a cluster; no entry-side correction for this class is justified from this evidence. Monitoring continues.
- UNRESOLVED (look 3 with K = 2–3): the live rate lies between the two hypotheses or the data cannot tell; the interval is reported; no correction is justified by this test; a longer horizon would be declared anew.
## What cannot be concluded within this horizon
Causes. A rejection says the rate differs from history; it does not say whether feeds, settlement timing, market microstructure or chance clustering are responsible. Rates below p1 but above p0 (for example 0.2 %) cannot be distinguished from either hypothesis within 1,600 windows. Nothing here measures execution availability; that is reported separately (round5/exec/exec_assess.txt).
