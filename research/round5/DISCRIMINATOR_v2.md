# Pre-registered prospective discriminator, version 2 (written 2026-11-16T08:51:32Z by the clock; supersedes DISCRIMINATOR.md v1, which is kept; no window closing after the cut-off has been examined for entries or outcomes)
Why a version 2 before any data: v1 took its baseline from the replay cohort that section 20–22 of the record show is NOT comparable to the live cohort (0 in 7,281 at a stricter effective threshold). The comparable cohort (volatility reading at the live level, pre-registered, two methods agreeing) gives 4 in 9,621 over the whole of 2026 (0.042 %) and 3 in 2,165 over Sep 1–Nov 12 (0.139 %); the replay itself shows the mid-horizon loss rate roughly doubling from September (record section 22). The baseline below is the most recent comparable period, which is the harder hypothesis to reject; the full-year rate is reported alongside.
## Question
Is the live loss frequency of the owner's entry class at 90–120 s left higher than its comparable recent historical frequency, or were the losses of Nov 13–16 a chance cluster at that frequency?
## Eligible population and denominator (unchanged from v1)
One observation = one 15-minute market with an observer primary-rule v1 entry (first production /predictions row with 0 < tau ≤ 120 s, state LOCKED or PROVEN LOCKED, pFlip ≤ 0.002 inclusive; existing-lock, new-crossing and unknown-prior all count) whose entry tau satisfies 90.0 ≤ tau ≤ 120.0 s, whose outcome is a finalized Kalshi result, and whose window closes after 2026-11-16T07:00:00Z. Not-determinable, no-entry and tau < 90 s windows are not in the population. N = the count of such windows at each look, read from the observer's window lines; K = those reading 'signal WRONG'.
## Hypotheses (fixed)
H0: p ≤ p0 = 0.14 % (the comparable Sep 1–Nov 12 rate, 3 in 2,165). Reference rates, not used for decisions: full-year comparable 0.042 % (4 in 9,621); cushion-matched (≥ 15 bps, the live composition) Sep–Nov 0.064 % and full-year 0.013 %; the original non-comparable 0 in 7,281. H1: p ≥ p1 = 0.5 %, the LOCKED threshold (the "≤ 0.2 %" reading wrong by the whole LOCKED margin; the live Nov 2–16 rate 2 of 417 = 0.48 %).
## Looks, horizon and decision rule (fixed; verified by calculation and 300,000-run simulation, DISCRIMINATOR_v2_check.txt)
Looks when N first reaches 400, 800 and 1,600 eligible windows (about 14, 28 and 55 days at ~29 eligible entries a day). K = cumulative losses.
- Look 1 (N = 400): REJECT H0 if K ≥ 3 [P(K ≥ 3 | p0) = 0.019; P(K ≥ 3 | p1) = 0.32]. Otherwise continue.
- Look 2 (N = 800): REJECT H0 if K ≥ 4 [0.027 | 0.57]. Otherwise continue.
- Look 3 (N = 1,600): REJECT H0 if K ≥ 6 [0.027 | 0.81]; ACCEPT H0's adequacy if K ≤ 2 [P(K ≤ 2 | p1) = 0.014]; K = 3, 4 or 5 = UNRESOLVED and the test ends with the rate and its interval reported.
Overall (simulation): under H0 the test rejects with probability 0.052, accepts 0.61, is unresolved 0.34; under H1 it rejects 0.84, accepts 0.014, is unresolved 0.15. The thresholds will not be changed after a loss. No other look decides; the readings report K and N only.
## Previously inspected data
Everything up to the window closing 2026-11-16T07:00Z (Nov 2–16: 417 entries, 2 losses at [90,120]) was examined before both versions and is excluded. The [90,120] bin was chosen after seeing where the losses fell; this is its first clean test.
## What each outcome justifies
- REJECT: the live rate exceeds even the recent comparable history; the ≤ 0.2 % reading at 90–120 s is not supported live. Justified then: an entry-side correction for this class, declared and evaluated under a frozen protocol before use (PR10 stays rejected and is not revived), and the owner's own choice about entering this class meanwhile. Not justified: any change claimed to be validated by the rejection itself.
- ACCEPT: the live rate is consistent with the recent comparable history at the 0.5 % level; the Nov 13–16 losses are treated as a cluster; no entry-side correction is justified from this evidence; monitoring continues. Note that "consistent with 0.14 %" is itself close to the break-even loss frequency of a 99.8 ¢ purchase (0.18 % at 100 contracts): acceptance is not a statement that the class is profitable.
- UNRESOLVED: the rate lies between the hypotheses or the data cannot tell within 1,600 windows; the interval is reported; no correction is justified by this test; a longer horizon would be declared anew.
## What cannot be concluded within this horizon
Causes (feed, settlement timing, microstructure, chance). Rates between 0.14 % and 0.5 % cannot be separated from either hypothesis in 1,600 windows (34 % unresolved under H0). Whether the live engine's lower volatility reading is the better estimate of the settlement index's volatility is not tested here; execution availability is reported separately (round5/exec/exec_assess.txt).
