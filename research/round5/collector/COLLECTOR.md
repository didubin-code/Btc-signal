## 2. The existing session-independent collector: the observer (verified from its source and its Render logs)
* Runs on Render (pinradar-observer, autoDeploy off, deploys from the development branch by a manual deploy only), read-only GET client with an explicit path allowlist, no credentials, no redirects, response size cap.
* Storage: append-only, hash-chained, compressed, encrypted (to `keys/owner.public.pem`) segment store on a 10 GB persistent disk; nothing deleted; 1,072.4 MB after 16 days (≈ 2.7 MB/hour, ≈ 67 MB/day → 80 % of the disk in about 110 more days at this rate; the host logs a warning at 80 %).
* Sequence/gap checks: `obs.gap` (outage or dropped records), `obs.writefail`, `obs.stall`, production restarts and clock steps (`pr.restart`, `pr.clockstep`), ledger chain breaks, 120-s overlap re-reads of `/predictions`, torn-tail quarantine, anchors and signed checkpoints; `derive` recomputes coverage per window from the records.
* Failure reporting: `render_host.js` pings a dead-man's-switch URL every minute and `<url>/fail` when the disk is missing, over 80 % full or collection has stalled — IF a heartbeat URL is configured (an environment variable I cannot read; no `[host]` line is in the retained logs). Render also e-mails on deploy failures. Whether the owner runs the daily `backup`/`restore-test` the README asks for is not visible to me.
* Impact on production: GET `/predictions` every 30 s, `/health` every 60 s, signals/ledger every 60 s, books 1 Hz in the final 300 s — the load production has carried since Oct 31.
* Limit: the store is readable only with the owner's private key (on the owner's Mac). My analyses have used the observer's window LINES (Render logs, 14-day retention) and my own plaintext hourly tape pulls, which depend on this session.
