**Defects and observations in the original (`round3/exec/exit_eval.py`).**
1. L45 with L118-122: a partial fill is priced as if the whole size had filled (average over the filled part, remainder ignored). Corrected by `--fill-remainder` (remainder at 100/0, exit fee on the filled part, per-size nets). No effect on this data: 0 partial fills in 132 book exits at every size; the live books hold thousands of contracts at the bid.
2. L144 (comment L131-132): the "pessimistic" value of an unpriced winner exit is hold - 1.0 - fee(ask) = (100 - ask) - 1 - 2 fee, which is neither "1.0 c below the entry ask" (-1 - 2 fee) nor the hold outcome. Harmless so far (0 unpriced exits in both recorded runs; 1 in today's data: a market with neither prints nor book). `--books-only` prints the hold-outcome convention (win for a winner, full loss for a loss) beside the original formula.
3. L75/L81, data race: the recorded replay run read a trades file while the pull was still writing it (item above); the recorded "1 excluded" is an artifact of timing, not of the data.
4. L32, fee: float rounding adds one cent at sizes 100 and 1,000 for round prices (n 100, p 10: 63.000000000000001 -> 64 c; also p 20/40/50/60/70/80 at 100, p 70 at 1,000); impossible at size 10 (tested for every price 0.1-99.9); 0 occurrences among the 1,626-3,484 fee evaluations of these runs (header "fee check"). Frozen formula kept.
5. L9-13, close_ts: tickers with an "EST" suffix for the DST-ambiguous hour (`...NOV010100EST-00` to `...0145EST-45`) parse one hour early. None is in an evaluated set (the replay path uses the series' closeTs; the live set is Nov 12-15); close_ts equals Kalshi's closeMs for all 107 tape tickers and the live rows' ts + tau to within 5 ms.
6. L36: rebuilds the per-ticker timestamp list on every call (performance only; v2 indexes once, same bisect result). L119-120 (declared, not a defect): with no book within 2.5 s or no bid on the held side the exit falls back to prints, which show a trade, not depth, hence `--books-only`.
7. Readings, not code: P3's "<= 15 % of entries" clause fails on set (b) (37 of 238 = 15.5 %; owner-like 35 of 227 = 15.4 %) although RESULT_R3 records P3 as met there; P1 says "measured on prints" while the live losses are book-priced (both sources agree here: Nov 13 prints 99.9 at +8.5 s, Nov 15 prints below 95 from +21.2 s).
