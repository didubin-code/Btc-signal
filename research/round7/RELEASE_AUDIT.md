Realistic action: a person reacting in 3–5 s would still find the quoted offer in 4–5 of the 10 windows; in 10 s in 2. Quoted availability is not a guaranteed fill: the tape shows the resting book at 1 Hz, not fills; sizes are mostly large (median ≈ 69,000 at the best ask) so a 100–1,000-contract order would not be size-limited where the quote persists, but the quote can be pulled inside the second. Net: about 1 window in 17 has a quoted PR11 entry, about 1 in 35 survives a 5-s reaction, almost always at 99.9 ¢.
Contract-level result at the quoted price (default fee schedule, taker, C = 100): at 99.9 ¢, win +0.09 ¢, loss −99.91 ¢, break-even loss rate 0.090 % (C = 1,000: 0.093 %); at 99.8 ¢, win +0.18 ¢, break-even 0.180 %. If the true adverse rate were 0.025 % the expectation is about +0.06 ¢ per contract at 99.9 ¢; at 0.09 % it is zero; above it negative. PR11's value is prevention, not income.
The ≥ 5 bps exclusion: in the replay the 60 "recorded" prints are the proxy's, not the official BRTI's; the two series settle on different sides in 4.0 % of FIT and 6.3 % of TEST windows, all at margins of a few bps. Entries with raw cushions below about 5 bps (which PR11 admits only when ≤ 5 prints remain) therefore have outcomes in the replay that reflect the proxy–official basis, not the path risk the gate bounds. The floor is applied to baseline and PR11 alike; the all-entry official result is also reported (PR11 5 "losses" at cushions 1.0–2.7 bps and 3–9 s left, baseline 17) and the class is judged on live production rows, which record official prints: 61 PR11 entries below 5 bps, 0 lost (`round7/eval/live_sensitivity.txt`; upper 95 % bound 4.8 % — the live sample cannot bound this class tightly, and such seconds carry no offers in practice: 1 of the 10 executable windows is in the 0–10 s class).
What protection is supported, in the correct words: 0.05 % is the historical exceedance threshold used to build the table — the two-sided frequency, over 2023-10 … 2026-08, of a settlement-mean move at least as large as the floor. It is not a bound on future losses. Its own uncertainty with correlated seconds (`round7/qtable/q_uncertainty.txt`): pooled 0.046–0.050 % at the frozen floors; 7-day block-bootstrap 95 % intervals 0.035–0.065 %; distinct exceedance episodes 2,873 (h = 120) to 15,915 (h = 10) across 299–846 of 1,065 days; by year 0.033–0.068 %. Separated 2026 evidence consistent with it: TEST 0 of 4,452 official (≥ 5 bps; upper 0.067 %), 0 of 6,299 by path outcome (upper 0.0475 %), 0 of 4,456–4,457 on the two other cohorts, 0 of 85 on Nov 13–15; live 0 of 351 (upper 0.85 %, uninformative at this level). Repeated checking: the gate is evaluated every second, but the evaluation unit is the window (one entry per window), the table is one fixed table, and the criteria were fixed before the single evaluation pass; the known-incident prevention (C2) was foreseeable and is not counted as blind evidence. Zero losses on 2026 data do not mean zero losses in future; a move larger than any in the three-year record reverses a PR11 entry, and the November cluster shows regimes outside the recent record occur.
