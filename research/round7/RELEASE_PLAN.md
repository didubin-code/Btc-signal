## 3. Failure handling — precise
**Implementation failures (the software violates its gate, contradicts itself or fails operationally) — preauthorized rollback, executed by this session when seen, or by the owner at any time:**
- I1 gate violation: any tape or /predictions row with state LOCKED or PROVEN LOCKED, 0 < τ ≤ 120 s and pr11Admit false (C1). Response: rollback at once (Route A), report.
- I2 contradictory presentation or instruction: a TRADE NOW row whose state is not LOCKED/PROVEN (C3); a live render showing a green LOCKED ring, READY tile or TRADE NOW together with a withheld or not-yet-gated headline; a PROVEN headline without the conditional wording; a HIGH CONFIDENCE call that shows LOCKED statistics or a "LOCKED in ~N s" projection while withheld. Response: rollback (Route A), report.
- I3 operational: deploy not live within 15 min; /health unreachable for 5 min after live; identity mismatch (codeSha256, release, mode, table or declaration hash); a restart loop (more than 2 restarts in an hour in Render events); C2 (served LOCKED/PROVEN rows without pr11Admit); DATA UNRELIABLE on more than half the rows of two consecutive complete windows after the warm-up when the pre-deploy rate was below 10 %. Response: Route A for a configuration-level fix, Route B (revert) when the build itself is at fault or Route A fails; report.
- I4 the I2 conditions carried over: a stale episode of 45 s or more past the stale gate, or a window missing more than 90 s of decision time (outside the deploy window itself). Response: report at once; rollback if it recurs in 2 of 6 consecutive windows at a rate not seen before the deploy.
**Forecast outcomes (a correctly admitted call loses) — reported, decided by the owner, never an automatic rollback:**
- F1 one qualified entry loses in a complete window. Response: immediate report with the case (entry second, cushion and R vs floor, book, settlement, observer line) and a recommendation on pausing entries; PR11 stays on.
- F2 a second qualified entry loses within the first 2,000 qualified entries. Response: immediate report and the recommendation to stop treating LOCKED as an entry signal until reviewed; PR11 stays on unless the owner decides otherwise.
Which entries count: one per window — the first second with state LOCKED/PROVEN inside 120 s and pr11Admit true, in a complete window (final-120-s coverage ≥ 95 %); a later-admitted opposite side in the same window is recorded, not a second entry; mixed-build, incomplete, pending and unobserved windows are excluded from both numerator and denominator and listed. Rationale and limits of F2: at the historical exceedance frequency (≤ 0.05 % per admitted entry) the expected losses in 2,000 entries are ≤ 1; two or more losses then occur with probability 26 % (rate 0.05 %) or 9 % (0.025 %), one or more with 63 % / 39 %; a tenfold failure (0.5 %) gives two or more with probability above 99.9 %. The rule therefore catches gross failure only; it cannot confirm the historical level (a 95 % upper bound of 0.05 % needs about 6,000 loss-free entries); at ≈17 entries per 72 h, 2,000 entries take about a year. A monitoring threshold is not a guarantee of safety. Rollback does not help a forecast failure: switching PR11 off restores I2's less restrictive policy (LOCKED shown again at 15–20 bps cushions at 90–120 s, the Nov 14 / Nov 15 loss conditions) — it is not safer for reversal risk, which is why F-class triggers lead to a decision about entering at all, not to a rollback.
