## 2. Finding B — exposure units (corrected code: build 10)
The exposure table measures |mean(remaining prints) − p[t]| / p[t], bps of the CURRENT price; build 9 computed the required move as |gap| / strike. The recorded row KXBTC15M-26NOV152000-00 at 1794790749094 (50.906 s, strike 64,707.58, index 64,883.71): 32.0173 bps in strike units (admitted) versus 31.9304 in table units (withheld at the 32 floor) — reproduced (outputs/audit_reproduction/denominator_audit.json; outputs/chrono/denominator_regression_B9.txt). Settlement arithmetic: the mean of the h remaining prints must move 60·|gap|/h dollars from the current index for the settlement to cross the strike; expressed in the table's units that is 60·|gap|/h / index, so the index is the consistent denominator in both directions (an UP leader above the strike read too large in strike units, a DOWN leader below it too small). Smallest correction (sources/b9_to_b10.diff, 56 diff lines, 20 code lines): pr11Exposure divides by geo.index (the strike only if no finite index exists — never in production); the withheld text names the units. Thresholds, table and every other requirement unchanged. ## 4. Risk claims, corrected
Removed: "0.05 % two-sided ≈ 0.025 % adverse" and the "factor ≈ 3.6 below break-even". The table gives an unconditional, pooled, two-sided frequency over all seconds of 2023-10…2026-09 that the settlement-mean move exceeded R*(h); it conditions on nothing the policy selects on (LOCKED state, pFlip ≤ 0.2 %, a quoted offer) and divides by two only under a symmetry assumption; it does not establish the conditional loss probability of executable entries or any margin to the 0.09 % break-even. Also corrected: the frozen floor is pooled across volatility regimes, not a per-regime bound — by the engine-like bucket (outputs/table_v2/per_bucket_exceedance_at_frozen_floor.txt) the two-sided exceedance at R* is 0.0016–0.002 % in the calmest half of seconds, 0.04 % in bucket c3, 0.09–0.11 % in c4 (4 % of seconds), 0.27–0.42 % in c5 (2 %) and 2.7–4.1 % in c6 (1 %); the November losses fell in regimes 3–4. What the evidence supports: (a) regression — all three qualifying losses are not taken on their side (direct replay Nov 14, rows Nov 15, reconstruction Nov 11); (b) the separated proxy replay — 0 losses in 4,452 TEST entries with cushion ≥ 5 bps (upper 0.067 %), unchanged in table units, proxy-based and now reproducible from the package (trace, records, outcomes included); (c) the live record 0 of 402 (upper 0.74 %), consistent with (b) but unable to bound anything near the break-even. Provenance supplied: the Binance Vision 1-s kline archive (sources/provenance/PROVENANCE_binance_BTCUSDC.ndjson: every zip URL with its published checksum and fetched sha256), the fetch/convert script, the per-day .f64 hashes, the table builders (pooled build_q_full.py, bucketed build_q.py) and both tables; the 757 MB of 1-s closes themselves are not included but are rebuildable from those URLs. ## 5. Decision (deployment not requested; stop at the approval boundary)
Implementation correctness: build 9 had a units defect (fixed in build 10, verified by three independent routes); its lock memory behaves as the engine intends and is now scored; build 10 passes every test in hand and reproduces the served state exactly except at PR11-withheld seconds (0 unexplained differences). Demonstrated protection: supported as regression on the three losses and as a separated-replay frequency bound that the live record does not contradict; NOT a per-regime bound and NOT a demonstrated conditional loss probability for quoted entries. Practical trading usefulness: not established — 6 first-entry offers in 201 windows with a book, 9 opportunity windows of 212, almost all at 99.9 ¢, and no sample that can verify the 0.09 % break-even. Overall: **supported protective safeguard; practical trading usefulness unproven.** If the owner wants the safeguard live, the approval statement v8 (PR11_RELEASE.md) names build 10; nothing moves until it is written in the owner's words.
